## 📚 Phase 1 (Wk 1–4): Technical Vocabulary & Clarity

**Goal:** Sound like an AI engineer, not a student

**Daily habit (5 min):**

- Learn 2 new technical terms
- Use them in a sentence out loud
- Explain yesterday's term without notes

**Weekly habit:**

- Record yourself explaining the week's topic in 2 minutes
- Listen back — count filler words (um, uh, like, you know)
- Reduce filler words by 1 each week

**Core vocab to master — Month 1:**

LLM, token, context window, temperature, inference, embedding, vector, cosine similarity, system prompt, few-shot, chain-of-thought, RAG, chunking, retrieval, augmentation, tool use, function calling, JSON schema, prompt injection, hallucination, grounding

## 📝 Phase 2 (Wk 5–8): Structured Explanation

**Goal:** Explain anything clearly to any audience

### The BLUF Method (Bottom Line Up Front)

> **Bad:** "So basically what happens is, you take the text and then it goes through a tokenizer and then the model processes it..."

> **Good:** "The model converts text to tokens, processes them through transformer layers, then generates tokens one at a time."

### Techniques to practice:

1. 30-second explanation (elevator pitch)
2. 2-minute deep dive
3. Analogy-first explanation
4. Numbers & examples (never vague)

### The Feynman Technique (weekly):

- Pick one concept. Explain it as if teaching a complete beginner.
- Where you get stuck = where you don't truly understand.
- Go back and fill that gap.

## 🎤 Phase 3 (Wk 9–12): Interview Communication

**Goal:** Sound confident, structured, and senior

### The STAR Method

- **S**ituation: Set the scene briefly (1-2 sentences)
- **T**ask: What was your responsibility?
- **A**ction: What did YOU specifically do? (most important)
- **R**esult: Quantify if possible

### 20 Behavioral Questions to Prepare:

1. Tell me about yourself (2-minute pitch)
2. Why AI engineering?
3. Tell me about your most complex project
4. Tell me about a time you failed
5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone
6. Tell me about learning something difficult quickly
7. Why Anthropic/OpenAI/Google? (per company)
8. Tell me about a decision with incomplete information
9. What's your greatest weakness?
10. Tell me about a time you influenced without authority
11. Describe a project you're most proud of
12. Tell me about a time you improved a process
13. What if you disagreed with your manager?
14. Tell me about handling a tight deadline
15. What does good engineering look like to you?
16. Tell me about a time you helped a teammate
17. Where do you see yourself in 3 years?
18. What excites you about AI?
19. Tell me about a mistake and what you learned
20. Do you have questions for us? (always have 3 ready)

### Think-Aloud for Coding:

> When solving a coding problem, ALWAYS narrate:
> - "First, I'm going to understand the problem — let me restate it..."
> - "My initial approach is... but I see a potential issue with..."
> - "I'll start with brute force, then optimize..."
> - "The time complexity here is O(n) because..."

## 🏗️ Phase 4 (Wk 13–16): Architecture Communication

**Goal:** Sound like a senior engineer

- Draw while you talk (never just talk or just draw)
- Name every component explicitly
- State your assumptions first
- Offer tradeoffs proactively ("I chose X over Y because...")
- Ask clarifying questions before designing

**Handling "I don't know":**

- "I don't know, but here's how I'd find out..."
- "That's outside my experience, but based on similar patterns..."
- "Let me think step by step..." (then actually do it)

## 🏆 Phase 5 (Wk 17–24): Mock Interview Polish

### Full Interview Checklist:

**Before the interview:**

- Know the company's AI products deeply
- Have 3 questions ready to ask them
- Practice 2-minute "tell me about yourself"
- Every project pitchable in 2 min

**During the interview:**

- State assumptions before solving
- Think aloud throughout
- Ask clarifying questions
- State time/space complexity
- Test solution with an example
- Offer tradeoffs and alternatives

**After the interview:**

- Send thank-you email within 24 hours
- Note what to improve
- Research anything you didn't know
