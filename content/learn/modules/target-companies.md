---

## 🟣 Anthropic

**Interview Style:** Deep AI + safety thinking + practical coding

**Must Know:**

- Constitutional AI (read the paper summary)
- RLHF and how Claude is trained
- Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)
- How Claude differs from GPT architecturally
- Claude's Character and values

**Key Questions They Ask:**

- "How would you make an AI system safer?"
- "What do you think about AI x-risk?"
- "Walk me through Constitutional AI"
- "How would you evaluate if a model is safe?"

**Project to Build:** Something demonstrating safety-aware AI design

---

## 🟢 OpenAI

**Interview Style:** Agents, tools, fast iteration, capability + safety balance

**Must Know:**

- GPT architecture basics (transformer, attention)
- RLHF and fine-tuning process
- Multimodal models (GPT-4V, Whisper, DALL-E)
- Reasoning models (o1, o3 — how they differ)
- OpenAI API: assistants, tools, structured output

**Projects they love:** Agents, tool use, creative applications

---

## 🔵 Google / DeepMind

**Interview Style:** Heavy LeetCode + system design at Google scale

**Must Know:**

- Solve Hard LeetCode consistently
- Gemini architecture and products
- Vertex AI, Google Cloud AI stack
- "Design at Google scale" (billions of users)
- MapReduce, Bigtable mental models

**Key Difference:** Google has the hardest DSA bar of all target companies.

---

## 🔵 Meta AI

**Interview Style:** DSA + open-source AI knowledge

**Must Know:**

- Llama model family (Llama 2, Llama 3)
- PyTorch fundamentals
- FAISS (Facebook's vector search library)
- Open-source AI philosophy
- Meta's AI research areas (FAIR)

**Values:** Open source, fast iteration, scale

---

## 🟡 Sarvam AI

**Interview Style:** Practical AI + passion for India AI ecosystem

**Must Know:**

- Indic language AI challenges
- Low-resource NLP techniques
- Multilingual model architectures
- India's AI landscape and opportunities

**Project to Build:** Anything involving multilingual/Indic language processing

---

## 🟠 PayPal

**Interview Style:** Applied AI + software dev combo

**Must Know:**

- AI in fintech — fraud detection patterns
- Risk models and compliance AI
- Safety and guardrails for financial AI
- Combine your AI + software dev strength

**Key Angle:** Your software dev background + AI knowledge is a perfect fit

---

## ⚪ Syncfusion

**Interview Style:** Practical AI for developer tools

**Must Know:**

- AI-enhanced UI components
- Code generation for components
- Developer tools and workflows

**Project to Build:** Something with Syncfusion products + Claude API
