RPM (what you keep) runs ~30-50% below CPM. US/UK/CA/AU audiences earn 3-5x more than India on the same content.

## High-reach formats (grow fast; lower CPM, use as top-of-funnel)

Data-viz / bar-chart-race, quiz/trivia, did-you-know/facts/listicles, motivational, news explainers, STEM/exam-prep, and short-form. **Shorts RPM is only ~0.01-0.20 USD** — use Shorts for reach, long-form for RPM, and brands/affiliates/products for income.

## Tamil-specific opportunities (Tamil Nadu / Sri Lanka / diaspora)

Tamil + Telugu are the highest-paying Indian regional languages, with far less competition than English. Big, code-friendly niches: **share-market/finance in Tamil** (broker/Demat affiliates), **TNPSC / exam-prep** (sells PDFs + courses), **tech/gadgets in Tamil** (gadget affiliates), **news/current-affairs**, **devotional Shorts** (huge reach, low RPM -> funnel), and **quiz/GK**. Run bilingual (Tamil audio + English captions, Tanglish) to capture both the high-CPM global and the large Tamil/diaspora markets.

## Monetization timeline (verify at signup)

YouTube Partner Program = **1,000 subs + 4,000 watch-hours (12 mo)** OR **10M Shorts views (90 days)**; an early tier opens at **500 subs**. Faster day-one income: affiliate links (high-ticket finance/SaaS beat ad RPM at low views), digital products (eBooks, Notion templates, TNPSC notes), and brand deals.

## Your 6-step start plan

1. Pick **2 niches**: one money (finance or AI-tools) + one reach (quiz or data-viz). Run both bilingually.
2. Post **9:16 Shorts daily** for reach; **1-2 long-form 16:9 per week** for RPM.
3. **Template once, vary the data** (keep every video genuinely different).
4. Add **Tamil + English captions** (widens diaspora reach).
5. Monetize in layers: **affiliate -> digital products -> ads -> brand deals**.
6. Stay compliant with YouTube's 2025 inauthentic-content rule: code is the accelerator, your script/insight is the product.
