## 🎤 Practice Q&A

**Q: What should I post if I'm still learning and don't have much to share?**

> A: "Today I Learned" posts are your most powerful format as a beginner. Document your learning journey — the confusion, the aha moments, the experiments that failed. Authenticity outperforms expertise in the algorithm. A post like "I spent 3 hours debugging why ChromaDB returned empty results — here's what I missed" is gold for other learners and gets massive engagement.

**Q: How do I grow from 0 followers?**

> A: First 500 followers strategy: (1) Post every Monday and Thursday for 60 days — consistency > quality at the start. (2) Comment meaningfully on 3 posts by AI leaders daily — thoughtful comments on popular posts bring profile views. (3) Connect with 10 people per day in the AI space (engineers, not just founders). (4) Share your posts in relevant Discord/Slack communities. Takes about 60–90 days of consistency to hit 500.

**Q: What's the right posting frequency?**

> A: 2x per week is optimal for most people — enough to stay in the algorithm, not so much that quality drops. Never post something just to post. One high-quality post beats three mediocre ones. As you get more comfortable, 3x per week is the sweet spot for fast growth.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Posting and disappearing** — reply to every comment for the first 60 minutes after posting (algorithm rewards early engagement)
- **All posts, no engagement** — comment on others' posts daily; relationship-building > broadcasting
- **Generic motivational content** — "Work hard and you'll succeed" type posts harm credibility in a technical community
- **Ignoring the headline** — recruiters find you by searching keywords; your headline IS your SEO
- **Only posting when job searching** — start now, build an audience, so it's there when you need it
- **No call to action** — always end with a question or ask. LinkedIn posts with comments outperform those without by 3x
- **Photo-less profile** — profiles with a clear, professional photo get 14x more views
