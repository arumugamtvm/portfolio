**🔑 Offer Negotiation — 3 Rules That Work**

1. **Always negotiate.** The first offer is never the best offer. Even 10–20% more base salary is realistic and expected.
2. **Use market data.** "Based on [Levels.fyi](http://Levels.fyi) for this role in this city, the market range is ₹X–₹Y."
3. **Be specific and warm.** "I am genuinely excited about this opportunity. Can we discuss the base salary? I was expecting ₹X based on my research."
